01

Logo

The emblem is a compass of the day: night and notes on the left, sunrise and the road ahead on the right. It reads at large sizes; the wordmark and monogram carry the brand everywhere else.

PRIMARY LOCKUP — marketing, cover pages, splash
dayMarkable
HORIZONTAL LOCKUP — headers, docs
dayMarkable
REVERSED — the compass rose stands in for the emblem on dark
Clearspace

Keep clear space around every lockup equal to the height of the wordmark's "d". Nothing enters this zone — not taglines, not rules, not edges.

Minimum sizes

Emblem: 48px / 14mm. Below that, use the compass rose. Wordmark: 120px / 30mm wide. Never scale the primary lockup below 200px wide.

Don't

Don't recolor the emblem, set the wordmark in another typeface, add drop shadows, place the emblem on busy photography, or swap the gold/navy split of the wordmark.

02

Color

A paper-light system: parchment surfaces, midnight navy ink, and gold reserved for what matters — the marks, the actions, the next move.

Midnight
#1E2A44
Ink, headings, dark UI

#FDFAF3

Ratio. Parchment ~70%, Notepaper ~20%, Midnight ~8%, Gold ≤2%. Gold is punctuation, never a wall of color.
Accessibility. Body text is always Midnight on Parchment/Notepaper (11.9:1). Gold text only at 18px+ bold, or on Midnight.
